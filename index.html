iOS · 3D Gaussian Splatting

Gaussian splats, made on your iPhone.

Walk around an object, and Splatelier captures, trains and shows it as a 3D Gaussian Splatting scene. Everything runs on the device: no server, no upload, no desktop GPU.

Guided capture

See what you have covered while you capture.

Color fills in where you have been

As you walk around, the camera view turns white and fills in with splats wherever the phone has seen the surface, finer the closer you get. A gap is easy to spot and go back for. Frames that would come out motion-blurred are left out of training, and short animated tips on the start screen show how to move.

Mesh and editing

More than splats: a mesh, and the tools to tidy up.

A mesh from the same capture

Splatelier can turn a trained scene into a vertex-colored mesh: it fuses the Gaussians’ depth into a TSDF and extracts the surface. Show it with the splats or as plain shape in the viewer, measure on it, and export it as OBJ.

Crop with a box, sphere or lasso

Drag the handles of a box or sphere to keep or remove what is inside, or draw a loop on screen to cut through the scene. Crops only hide splats, so undo is instant.

Clear the floaters

Stray splats can hang in the air and hide the subject from some angles. Let Auto Detect find them, or tap them with a brush, then delete them together. Undo brings them back.

Grade the colors

Tint, temperature, saturation, brightness, black and white points and opacity. The grade is non-destructive: the trained data stays as it was, and Reset brings it back.

How it works

From a short walk-around to a 3D scene.

Capture

ARKit records images and camera poses. Blurred and badly exposed frames are left out, and a live preview shows which parts are covered.

Poses and seeds

Bundle adjustment refines the ARKit poses, with loop closures. Multi-view stereo turns the frames into dense seed points.

Train

The Gaussians are trained on the iPhone’s GPU with Slang kernels on MLX, slowing down when the phone gets hot.

View and export

Orbit, measure, crop and grade the result, make a mesh from it, and export the splats or the mesh in standard formats.

Features

Everything stays on the phone.

Guided capture

Blur and exposure filtering while you capture, a live splat preview of the coverage, and short animated tips.

On-device training

Pose refinement, dense seeds and training in one screen, with one progress bar and a picture that sharpens as it trains.

Metric scale

The scene keeps ARKit’s real-world scale, so distances you measure in the viewer are in meters.

Train more

Not sharp enough yet? Continue training a finished model from the viewer; the optimizer state is kept, so it picks up where it stopped.

Standard formats

Share 3DGS PLY, compressed PLY, SPZ, .splat, glTF (GLB), SOG, OpenUSD and the OBJ mesh. The Library also opens .ply, .splat and .spz files.

Mac tools

Retrain a capture on a Mac, train and evaluate on COLMAP scenes, and convert PLY files from the command line.

Get started

Build it with Xcode.

  • An iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or later. LiDAR is not required.
  • An Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 15 and Xcode 16 or later.
  • An Apple account added to Xcode, to sign the app.

Splatelier is an early release built from source; it is not on the App Store. See the README for signing and the full steps.
